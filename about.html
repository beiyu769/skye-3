Our Story

The Holiday Season That Broke Everything

The Pain

The founders of Haulmax started like most sellers do — shipping orders from a garage. Sales grew, so they outsourced to a 3PL. The holiday season was their best yet, until it wasn't: the 3PL couldn't ship on time. Gifts missed Christmas. Growth stalled.

When they showed up to salvage the season, they found a warehouse strewn with trash and demoralized staff who didn't care. That's when it clicked — fulfillment isn't a line item. It's the business.

The Fix

So they started their own 3PL. One built by operators who feel every late shipment and every lost unit as lost revenue — because they've lived it.

Today Haulmax runs 1.2M sqft of self-operated warehouses across 48 states, moving 3M orders a month. Same obsession, bigger footprint.

"What our competitors call shrink allowance, we call lost sales, profits, and customers. Since our mistakes will hurt you, we believe they should hurt us, too."
