Common Questions

You Asked. We Answered.

How fast can you receive and process my inventory?
We guarantee dock-to-stock in 48 hours. Most containers are received, broken down, and live in our WMS within 36 hours. Our 98% same-day container pickup rate and 20+ loading docks mean your freight is not sitting on a truck waiting for a dock door.
What platforms and marketplaces do you integrate with?
We have native integrations with Amazon (FBA and FBM), Walmart, eBay, Wayfair, TikTok Shop, SHEIN, Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, and Magento. For custom platforms, our engineering team builds API/EDI connections — typical turnaround is 2-3 weeks.
Do you really pay for lost or damaged inventory?
Yes. Our Zero Shrinkage Guarantee is contractual — not marketing. We reimburse lost or damaged goods at your retail value within 30 days. Our actual loss rate is under 0.1%, so we put our money where our mouth is.
What is your minimum order volume?
We work with brands processing 500+ orders per month. If you are just starting out, we recommend reaching out anyway — our account team can advise on the right timing and help you plan your logistics roadmap even if you are not ready to onboard today.
How do you handle peak season (Q4, Prime Day, etc.)?
We scale staffing and shifts 60-90 days ahead of major events. Our WMS includes capacity forecasting tools that alert us — and you — when volumes approach thresholds. During peak, we maintain the same SLA guarantees. No exceptions.
