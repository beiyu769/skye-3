Homestead Brands: 10X in 3 Years

Homestead Brands sells big, heavy, and bulky products that most 3PLs simply won't touch. After a frustrating search for a partner who "knows how to do exactly what we need," they found Haulmax.

With transparent pricing, personal attention, and a guarantee that puts real money on the line, Haulmax became the fulfillment backbone behind one of the fastest-growing D2C brands in its category.

The Challenge

Big, Heavy, and Hard to Ship

The Problem

Standard 3PLs and Shopify Fulfillment Network don't handle oversized inventory. Homestead's heavy D2C packages were falling through the cracks — and their former 3PL's support was "hit or miss: open a ticket, kick to offshore, circle the drain."

The Haulmax Solution

Native handling of 20+ lb parcels with no upper limit, transparent per-package pricing, and a US-based team that responds fast and fixes problems — then pays when something goes wrong. A partner that "raises the stakes for the whole company."
